If you are planning Thanksgiving or December travel, request your pet care as early as you can. An early request gives us time to talk through fit, check availability, and set up a meet and greet before you leave. Sending a request is free and needs no payment. Your final quote will depend on your dates, your pets, and the care you need.

### Why request early

A holiday request starts a conversation, not a checkout. I follow up personally about whether the care is a good fit, whether I can cover your dates, and what your exact quote will be. The sooner we start, the more room we have to work through those details before your trip gets close. I can't promise any particular day will be open, but starting early gives us the most room to look at options together.

Holiday plans also shift, so a request is not a confirmed booking. Nothing is locked in until we've agreed on the care, the dates, and the price.

### What to send

The details below make the first reply much more useful:

- **Service:** Tell me which care you're considering, such as [house sitting](/services/house-sitting) or [overnight dog sitting](/services/overnight-dog-sitting). If you're not sure, describe the routine you need.
- **Dates:** Your departure and return days, plus any days you need visits instead of an overnight stay.
- **Your home and town:** Fox River Grove and Cary are my core service area. Requests from other nearby towns are reviewed for travel time and availability.
- **Your pets:** Names, species, age, size, and temperament. Note any pets that should be kept apart.
- **Daily routine:** Feeding times, walk times, bedtime, and how each pet spends time alone.
- **Health and special needs:** A brief note is enough. Please don't write out medication schedules in the request. We'll talk through the details together before making plans. For any medical question, your pet's veterinarian is the right person to ask.
- **Access:** How I'll get in. Keep gate codes and keys out of the request and save them for a secure conversation after we confirm.
- **Weather sensitivities:** Tell me if your dog struggles in heat or cold, since that changes how we plan outdoor time.

### Holiday hazards to mention

Holiday meals bring extra risks for pets. Keep bones, fatty trimmings, and cooked leftovers out of reach, and tell your sitter about any foods that are off limits. Trash bags, string, and decorations can also tempt curious pets. If you think your pet has eaten something harmful, contact your veterinarian or an animal poison control service right away.
