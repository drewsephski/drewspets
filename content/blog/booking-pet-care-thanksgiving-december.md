Send your request as soon as you know your Thanksgiving or December travel dates, and include the details listed below. A request is free, comes with no obligation, and is not a confirmed booking, so you can start the conversation before anything is decided. I follow up personally about fit, availability, and an exact quote, usually with a meet & greet when it makes sense.

## Why ask early

Holiday weeks are busy for everyone, including the people who look after pets. Asking early gives us more time to check whether I can cover your dates, plan a meet & greet, and work through your pet's routine before you leave.

A request sent a few days before departure leaves less room for those conversations. It also leaves less time to settle questions about pick-up times, feeding, and where your pet likes to rest. I can't promise availability for any particular week, so the sooner we talk, the more room we have to find a good fit.

## What to send with your request

- Your dates: departure and return days, plus any drop-off or pick-up times
- Your pets: names, ages, and a sentence about each one's temperament
- Your routine: meals, walk times, favorite resting spots, and any house rules
- Any extra needs: say briefly if a pet needs more help, and we'll talk through the details together. You don't need to write out medication schedules in the form.
- Your location: Fox River Grove and Cary are my core service area. If you live in another nearby town, mention it. Requests from Barrington, Crystal Lake, Algonquin, Lake in the Hills, Huntley, Wauconda, Island Lake, and Lake Barrington are reviewed for travel time and availability.

The more specific you are, the easier it is to tell whether a plan will work. A clear routine helps me see what your pet needs, and it helps you compare what you hear back.

## How quotes work

Every quote depends on your dates, your pets, and the care they need. Holiday weeks, extra pets, visit length, and special care can all change the total. House sitting starts at $55 per night, and drop-in visits start at $22 per visit. Boarding and daycare are personally quoted, so I'll talk through the details with you before giving a price.

Holiday rates, extra pets, and special care are confirmed in advance, so they're settled before you pay. Nothing is paid when you send a request. We agree on the care and the price first, and then I send payment details. For a wider look at how pricing works, read our [pet sitting rates guide](/guides/pet-sitting-rates).

## Which service fits a holiday trip

The right choice depends on how long you're away and what your pet needs each day. [House sitting](/services/house-sitting) includes an overnight stay in your home and an agreed daytime routine. It is not continuous 24-hour supervision, so we'll discuss time alone and any extra visits before confirming.

Drop-in visits suit shorter absences, or a pet who is happiest at home with a midday check-in. You can mention preferred visit times and how often you'd like visits in your request.

[Boarding](/services/boarding) is overnight care away from your home, and every request is reviewed individually for fit and availability. If you're considering it, bring your pet's normal food, labeled medications, a leash, and a familiar bed or blanket. We'll confirm the full packing list and care arrangements before the stay.

## Keep the holiday table in mind

Holiday meals are a common source of trouble for pets. Let your sitter know which foods are off limits, and keep leftovers, bones, and fatty scraps out of reach. Guests coming and going can also mean more open doors, so tell me which doors and rooms need to stay closed.

If your pet eats something they shouldn't have, contact your veterinarian right away for guidance. For anything medical, your pet's own vet is the right person to ask, and I'll follow the instructions you give me.

If you'd like to prepare your dog for a new face at the door, [our holiday prep guide](/blog/prepare-dog-for-holiday-pet-sitter) walks through the routines that help a dog settle with a sitter.

## Before you confirm

Plan a meet & greet before the first stay whenever we can. It gives your pet a chance to meet me, and it gives you a chance to ask anything on your mind. We'll also agree on how you'd like to receive updates before the booking starts, so you know what to expect while you're away.

When your travel dates are set, send your request through [the booking page](/book?service=house-sitting) and tell me what you have in mind. The earlier you reach out, the more time we have to plan together.
