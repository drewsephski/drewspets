Request your Thanksgiving or December pet care as soon as your travel dates are firm. Send the service you want, your dates, and a short description of your pets, and I will follow up personally about fit, availability, and an exact quote. A request is free, carries no obligation, and needs no payment. Sending one early simply gives us more time to get the details right before you leave.

## Why an early request helps

Holiday weeks are often busy for travelers, so I would rather hear from you sooner than later. An early request gives us room to:

- Talk through your pet's routine, walk times, and any special needs before the trip starts
- Arrange a meet and greet, a good way for us both to get comfortable before care begins
- Settle the details that shape the price, such as dates, number of pets, and visit length

Early does not mean locked in. A request is not a confirmed booking, and I still need to check fit and availability before anything is agreed. Think of an early request as a way to start the conversation, not as a reservation.

## What to include in your request

[Fox River Grove and Cary](/locations) are my core service area. Requests from nearby towns are reviewed for travel time and availability, so include your city or ZIP code so I can check coverage quickly. The table below shows which details help most.

| Detail | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Service and dates | Shows what you need and when, so I can check timing |
| City or ZIP | Confirms coverage and travel time |
| Your pets | Names, ages, and how many pets are in the home |
| Daily routine | Meals, walk times, favorite beds, and bedtime habits |
| Special care | A brief note is enough; we talk through the details together |
| Update preference | Photos and messages are part of the care, so say how often you would like them |

Keep your notes short. You do not need to write out a full medication schedule in the form. If your pet needs extra help, mention it briefly, and we will talk through the details before making any plans. For anything medical, your pet's own veterinarian should guide the plan, and I will be honest about what I can safely help with.

Routine details matter more than most owners expect. A calm cat and a young, bouncy dog need very different plans, and so does a dog who sleeps at the foot of your bed compared with one who settles in a crate. The more specific you are about what your pet does through a normal day, the easier it is to set up the first visit well.

If you need overnight company at home, you can start with [house sitting](/services/house-sitting) and add any other details in your message.

## How quotes work

Starting rates give you a sense of where pricing begins. They are not final quotes:

- House sitting starts at $55 per night
- Overnight dog sitting starts at $55 per night
- Puppy care starts at $60 per night
- Dog walking starts at $22 per walk
- Drop-in visits and cat sitting start at $22 per visit
- Boarding and doggy day care are personally quoted

Final quotes depend on dates, pets, and care needs. Holidays, extra pets, visit length, and special care can all change the total, and I confirm an exact quote before you book. For a fuller explanation of how rates are set, read our [pet sitting rates guide](/guides/pet-sitting-rates).

### When payment comes up

Sending a request costs nothing and does not commit you to anything. We talk first, agree on the care and the price, and only then do I send payment details. Holiday rates, extra pets, and special care are confirmed in advance, so the number is settled before care starts rather than after you return.

## Getting your pet ready

Booking is one part of holiday planning. Your pet also needs time to adjust to a sitter in the home, and the routine side of that is worth starting before the trip. For practical steps you can take at home, read [how to prepare your dog for a holiday pet sitter](/blog/prepare-dog-for-holiday-pet-sitter).

Some general steps apply to nearly every pet. Keep their normal food and daily routine as steady as you can, make sure any ID tags and contact information are current, and leave clear notes about anything a sitter should know. If your trip overlaps Thanksgiving week, a request sent now leaves more time for the conversation than one sent the week before you leave.

## Timing for your trip

A simple order helps:

1. Send your request with your dates and pet details.
2. We talk about fit, routine, and a meet and greet if one is needed.
3. You receive an exact quote, and we agree on the care.
4. Payment details follow once the care and price are agreed.

When your dates are set, [send your holiday request](/book) and I will follow up personally.
